Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what personal information Broutelogistics ("we", "us") collects through this website and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Shipment tracking. When you look up a shipment, the tracking number you enter is sent to our database to return its status. We do not ask you to create an account to track a shipment.
  • Enquiry form. If you contact us, we collect your name, email address, optional phone number, selected service and your message.
  • Shipment records. To deliver shipments, our staff record details such as goods description, origin, destination and sender and recipient names and contact details. These are not shown on the public tracking page.
  • Technical data. Our hosting and database providers may process IP addresses and basic request logs for security and reliability.

How we use it

We use your information to provide tracking, respond to enquiries and quotes, carry out shipments, keep our systems secure and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

Cookies and storage

The public website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Staff sign-in uses temporary browser session storage that is cleared when the tab is closed.

Service providers

We use third-party providers to host the website and our database. These providers process data on our behalf. If you use the chat assistant, your message may be processed by an AI service provider to generate a reply; please do not include sensitive personal information in chat messages.

Retention

We keep enquiries and shipment records only as long as needed for the purposes above and to meet legal, tax and accounting requirements.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to object to certain processing. To make a request, contact us using the details below.
